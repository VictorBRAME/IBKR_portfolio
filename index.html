A portfolio built to be replicable

Large fund managers hold many positions and often own very expensive shares, which makes replication impossible for most individuals. Nevertheless, to avoid paying excessive fees, low broker costs and a sufficient position size must be taken into account. An amount of around 5,000 USD is therefore enough to keep fees low, even though the portfolio can be replicated with much less.

The portfolio follows a value strategy, sometimes buying on exotic markets. Following it therefore requires a broker with wide geographic access, such as Interactive Brokers (IBKR), where the portfolio is held.

Our vision

As of Nov 2, 2026

We expect inflation to remain elevated over the coming years, for two main reasons.

1. Energy supply is fragile. The world still depends on fossil fuels for 86% of its energy: oil (33%), coal (28%) and natural gas (25%). Yet years of underinvestment and fast-declining oil fields leave production with little room to respond, and new capacity takes years to build. The war in the Middle East has shown how quickly prices react when supply is disrupted:

  • Disruptions to oil production and shipping have been offset so far by record releases from strategic reserves, which cannot last indefinitely and will eventually need to be refilled.
  • Strikes on refineries in the Gulf and in Russia have lifted the price of refined products, which feed directly into consumer prices.

2. High public debt. Government debt keeps rising. Over time, this creates pressure to tolerate inflation above bond yields, as after the Second World War, when negative real rates eroded the real value of government debt and the purchasing power of savers.

The portfolio is therefore built to protect purchasing power and grow capital in an inflationary environment. Stock selection follows two main criteria: companies that benefit from supply-driven inflation, which is why energy is at the core of the portfolio, and value criteria such as low valuation multiples, strong free cash flow and solid balance sheets.

The portfolio can stand on its own or serve to diversify an existing savings allocation.
